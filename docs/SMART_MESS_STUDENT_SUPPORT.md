# SMART MESS — STUDENT SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Welcome to SMART MESS (Official Version 9.2.0). If you experience any difficulties installing or using the app, please refer to the solutions below.

---

## Quick Troubleshooting

### 1. App Does Not Install ("App not installed" / Signature Conflict)
- **Cause**: An older testing or debug build of SMART MESS is already installed on your device.
- **Solution**: For devices upgrading from official 9.1.0, install `SMART_MESS_9.2.0_OFFICIAL.apk` directly as an update. If you previously had an unofficial debug build, uninstall it first from settings, then install `SMART_MESS_9.2.0_OFFICIAL.apk`.
- **"Blocked by Play Protect"**: Tap "More details" ➔ "Install anyway".

### 2. Login Fails / "Invalid Credentials"
- **Solution**: Ensure you are on the **Student** tab. Enter your registered hostel Roll Number (e.g. `25cy089` or `TEST2026`) and password carefully (passwords are case-sensitive).

### 3. "Server Unavailable" Banner
- **Cause**: Temporary loss of internet connection or Render backend cold-start.
- **Solution**: Check your Wi-Fi or 4G/5G mobile data. The app will automatically reconnect as soon as network connectivity is restored.

### 4. Reservation Does Not Update
- **Solution**: Pull down on the reservation screen to refresh or switch between the Today/Tomorrow tabs. Ensure your device time is set to Automatic (IST / Asia-Kolkata).

### 5. Fingerprint / One-Tap Login Unavailable
- **Cause**: Fingerprint hardware is disabled, un-enrolled, or cancelled.
- **Solution**: Ensure your fingerprint is registered in your phone’s system security settings. If your device does not have fingerprint hardware, use the manual credentials login.

### 6. Food Feedback Fails
- **Solution**: Ensure you have selected a star rating (1 to 5 stars). Comments are optional. If network is slow, wait 2–3 seconds for the confirmation message.

### 7. Photo Upload Fails
- **Solution**: When prompted, allow camera/storage permissions. If an image is extremely large, try taking a fresh photo directly with the camera.

### 8. Internet Unavailable (Offline Mode)
- **Solution**: SMART MESS allows you to view your cached meal schedule even when offline. Meal bookings made offline will be queued and synchronized automatically once internet returns.

### 9. Forgot Password / Password Issue
- **Solution**: On the Login screen, tap "Forgot Password", or if already logged in, tap the top-right Settings icon ➔ "Change Password". You may also contact the Mess Warden to verify your account status.

### 10. App Closes Unexpectedly
- **Solution**: Restart the app. SMART MESS includes automatic session recovery and will restore your account state safely.

---

## Reporting Serious Issues
If a problem persists, please report it to the Mess Office or your hostel student representative with:
1. **Phone Model** (e.g., Samsung Galaxy A34, Redmi Note 13, TECNO POVA 7)
2. **Android Version** (e.g., Android 14)
3. **Exact Time** the issue occurred
4. **Feature Used** (e.g., Lunch Reservation, Photo Feedback)
5. **Screenshot** of the error screen (if possible)

> **IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTE**: Never share your account password, PIN, or verification codes with anyone. The SMART MESS support team will NEVER ask for your password.
