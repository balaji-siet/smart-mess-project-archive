# SMART MESS — MESS SUPERVISOR OPERATIONAL GUIDE

This guide provides mess managers and supervisors with clear instructions on managing daily operations using the SMART MESS Supervisor Dashboard (Version 9.2.0).

---

## 1. Supervisor Login & Security
- Open the SMART MESS application.
- Tap the **Supervisor** tab at the top of the login screen.
- Enter your registered Supervisor Employee ID / Email and password.
- Tap **Sign In**.
- *Security Note*: The Supervisor interface is strictly role-isolated. Student accounts can never access Supervisor tools.

---

## 2. Live Mess Demand & Planning
- **Demand Cards (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)**:
  - Displays the exact number of students who have reserved each meal for Today and Tomorrow.
  - Kitchen staff must use these numbers for raw material preparation to ensure zero food waste.
- **Total Reservations**:
  - Live count of active bookings across all meal types.

---

## 3. Menu Management
- Navigate to **Menu Management** from the dashboard.
- View today’s active menu or select future dates using the calendar.
- Review Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner items to ensure accurate food display for students.

---

## 4. Reservation Schedule & Cutoff Times
- Open **Reservation Schedule**.
- Review cutoff times for each meal:
  - Breakfast Cutoff (e.g. 07:00 AM)
  - Lunch Cutoff (e.g. 11:30 AM)
  - Dinner Cutoff (e.g. 06:30 PM)
- Students cannot book or cancel meals after the cutoff time passes.

---

## 5. Live Attendance & Student Tracking
- **Live Attendance**: View students currently checked in at the mess counters in real-time.
- **Non-Attending Students**: Access the list of students who booked meals but have not yet checked in. Enables mess administration to track attendance compliance.
- **Student Management**: Search students by Roll Number, view hostel room allocation, and review reservation history.

---

## 6. Feedback & Food Quality Review
- Navigate to **Food Feedback** / **Feedback History**.
- Review student star ratings, comments, and submitted food photos.
- Tap any student photo to open the high-resolution view for kitchen quality audits.

---

## 7. Reports & Waste Analytics
- **Attendance Reports**: Generate daily and weekly attendance trends and export CSV reports.
- **Waste Analytics**:
  - Calculates unused meals: `(Reserved Meals - Actual Attendance)`.
  - Displays food savings percentages and sustainability metrics.

---

## 8. Connection Diagnostics & Logout
- **Connection Diagnostics**: Check real-time API latency and database connection status.
- **Logout**: Always log out when sharing or leaving a supervisor terminal or mobile device unattended.
